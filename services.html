Our method

How a project actually runs

Five stages, each with a defined deliverable and a payment milestone attached to it. Nothing moves forward until you sign off the stage before.

Discovery

A free 90-minute site visit. We measure, photograph and agree the brief, the budget band and the programme.

Concept

Space plans, mood boards and a material palette. Two directions, then one is refined with you.

Detailing

Working drawings, joinery sections, lighting layouts, and an itemised bill of quantities you approve line by line.

Execution

Demolition, civil work, MEP, joinery and finishing under our own supervision, with weekly photo reports.

Handover

Styling, deep clean, joint snag walk, warranty documents and a printed care guide for every finish.
