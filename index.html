Who we are

A Lahore studio that designs, builds and hands over.

Most interior firms stop at a mood board. We stay until the last light switch is on. Aurelia was founded in 2013 on a simple idea: the people who draw a room should be accountable for how it is built.

That means our designers sit twenty feet from our carpenters, our project directors visit every site weekly, and the bill of quantities you approve on day one is the bill you settle at handover. It is a slower way to grow a practice, and a far better way to finish a room.

  • Fixed, itemised quotations — no mid-project surprises or hidden variation claims.
  • Our own 9,000 sq ft workshop in Sundar Industrial Estate, with fourteen full-time craftsmen.
  • A 5-year workmanship warranty on everything we build and install.

How it works

Four steps from first call to handover
