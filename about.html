Our story

It started because a client asked a fair question.

“You have drawn me a beautiful room — so why can't you also build it?”

In 2013 Zohaib Rahman was three years out of the National College of Arts, drawing interiors that other people then executed badly. Drawings would be misread, sections ignored, a 40 mm reveal built at 90 mm. The gap between the design and the delivery was where every project lost its quality.

So Aurelia was set up to close that gap. First one carpenter, then a small joinery unit, and by 2019 a 9,000 sq ft workshop with fourteen craftsmen on staff. Today we draw it, we build it, and we are the ones who answer the phone if something is wrong two years later.

We are deliberately not the biggest studio in Lahore. We take on roughly thirty projects a year because that is the number our directors can personally walk every week.

Zohaib Rahman — Founder

What guides us

Mission, vision and the rules we won't bend

Our mission

To make well-designed, properly built interiors normal in Pakistan — not a luxury reserved for a handful of addresses in Lahore and Karachi.

Our vision

To be the studio Pakistani homeowners recommend without being asked, and the first name on a developer's tender list for interiors that must actually last.

Our promise

A fixed price before we start, a weekly photograph from your site, and a five-year warranty on every joint we cut. In writing, every time.

Honesty over upselling

If your kitchen only needs new fronts and lighting, we will tell you that, even though a full rebuild pays us more.

Craft kept in-house

Fourteen full-time carpenters and finishers, paid monthly, trained by us. Not a rotating cast of daily-wage subcontractors.

Responsible sourcing

Seasoned, legally harvested hardwood, low-VOC finishes and local artisans wherever the specification allows it.

Time is part of the brief

A programme is issued at kick-off and updated every Friday. If a date slips, you hear it from us first.
